## 9. Performance risks (read-only; nothing measured)

Facts on `SK_Warden` and `CharacterMesh0`:
- `visibilityBasedAnimTickOption` is AlwaysTickPose (not OnlyTickPoseWhenRendered): a dormant hidden Warden still evaluates a 110-bone clip. `bEnableUpdateRateOptimizations` is false, so it runs at full rate at any distance.
- Shadows: `castShadow` and `bCastDynamicShadow` true, `bCastContactShadow` true; it casts into every shadow-casting light in range. `TellLight` is shadowless.
- `bSupportRayTracing` true and `rayTracingMinLOD` 0: with hardware Lumen (the project setting per MCP_NOTES) the skinned mesh is in the ray-tracing scene at LOD0 and its BLAS is updated every frame while it animates.
- LODs: 99,974 / 49,975 / 19,973 triangles; source-model screen sizes 1.0 / 0.5 / 0.25, hysteresis 0.02, no minLod, not Nanite, no LOD streaming. By my reading of the screen-size rule, LOD0 is used only within roughly 3.5 to 6 m and LOD2 beyond roughly 7 to 12 m (depends on aspect ratio and FOV; **an estimate, unverified**). So the owner 3 m reading is the LOD0 worst case, the 8 m reading probably LOD1 or LOD2.
- Textures: 6 at 4096 squared (wool and skin sets), 2 at 1024, 12 at 2048; about 130 MiB by the script estimate (all mips); streaming allowed (`NeverStream` false). `M_Warden_Wool` is two-sided with detail maps.
