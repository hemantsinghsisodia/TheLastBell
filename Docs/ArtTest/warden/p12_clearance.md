# P12-5 clearance: how much headroom the Warden has on its routes (measured for V5, height 290 cm)

**V5.5 note (P12-9):** the numbers stand, because the 292 cm limit used here still covers V5.5: Idle top 285.6, Walk top 290.1, so Walk has 1.9 cm to spare against the limit. Crypt stair ends (286, 271, 256) clip Walk by about 4, 19 and 34 cm, so Phase 13 needs the stoop there.

Generated by `Tools/warden/measure_clearance.py` (read-only; re-run it to regenerate). Limit: **292 cm** (V5 Idle tops out at 290.3, Walk at 291.8).

## How to read the numbers
- Traces cannot find the ceilings (the art has no collision), so every number comes from the layout records `Docs/plans/p9_d1_layout.json` (crypt) and `p10_d1_layout.json` (cloister, tower), measured from the floor.
- **conservative** = the lowest AABB bottom / springing of any ceiling record over the point. A vault's or arch's bounding box starts at its springing, which is lower than the curved surface above most of the floor, so this number is a floor under the real clearance.
- **estimated** = the underside at the point where the profile is known (crypt `SM_Roof` bay from the P9 survey grid, crypt `SM_Arch` portals from the survey's traced table, cloister arcade arches from their geometry). **The crypt vault profile is modelled from the survey's sample points, not measured on the mesh.** Where no profile is known (cloister barrel vault, the two archways, beams, boards) estimated = conservative.
- **crown** = the highest intrados the data gives over the point (vault crown +2 from its pivot; arch crown; barrel-vault apex ~ springing + 313).
- Status: BELOW = estimated < 292; SPRINGING-ONLY = only the conservative number is below 292; ok = both at or above; NO-DATA = no ceiling record over the point (never counted as clear).
- **greybox trace up** = a vertical `trace_world` from floor+5 cm, first hit within 1500 cm. The crypt, cloister and tower greybox shells keep their (hidden) collision, so this is what the Warden's capsule actually meets; it sees collision only, never the art. 'open' = nothing within 1500 cm.
- Floor = the markers' own Z (crypt -600, cloister 0). Route segments are straight lines between waypoints (and last -> first, the route loops), sampled every 100 cm; the walked path follows the navmesh through the portals, so some samples lie in walls, over the pit or in the garth. They are marked. Three segments whose straight line leaves the rooms (crypt 4->5 and the loop 7->1 cross solid rock, the cloister loop 7->1 crosses the garth) are sampled along an **assumed** path through the portal openings (J8, J10; J7, J6, J5) or round the walk corner (9850, 900); the segment name says so.

## Could not be determined
- **Crypt vault underside between the survey samples is modelled** (blend of the diagonal and edge profiles); the mesh has no collision and no vertex readout exists. The conservative numbers do not depend on it. Where the estimate is within ~40 cm of 292 the number should be treated as unverified; none of the route samples is that close unless listed above.
- The cloister barrel vault's lower surface is taken as its springing (540) everywhere; the true intrados is higher, up to ~853 at the apex.
- **Dressing is not treated as a ceiling**: hanging roots (`RootsHang`), chains, lanterns on brackets, cobwebs, the moon-grate collars (bottom -62 = 538) were not checked against the head.
- Straight-line sampling: wall, pit and garth samples are marked in the appendix; the true walked path was not computed (no navigation query is available through the tools).
- NO-DATA samples are listed below and are not counted as clear.
