# Generated audio: audition page (A4)

Generated by `Tools/audio/gen/build_all.py` (`python -I Tools/audio/gen/build_all.py --group all`). Open each WAV on headphones. For every cue decide **keep**, **redo** (say what to change) or **replace** (with a Freesound CC0 sound). Spectrogram sheets: `gen_<group>.png` next to this page.

**How to read the columns.** *RMS* / *Peak* are measured on the written 16-bit file. *Seam* is the loop wrap: RMS of the first 0.5 s against the last 0.5 s (target under 0.5 dB); *click* is the sample jump at the wrap divided by the typical sample jump (about 1-3 is normal, above 8 would click). *Mix gain* is the intended in-game level relative to the file (section 4 'Level' values); every file is normalised the same way (beds -24 dBFS RMS, one-shots -3 dBFS peak, music -22 dBFS RMS), so play the bell stages with this offset in mind: stage 1 is meant to be 18 dB quieter than stage 5.

Note: dBFS here is digital level, not loudness; turn the volume down before the thunder, the strikes, the bell stages 4 and 5 and the stings.
