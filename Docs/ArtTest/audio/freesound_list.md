# Freesound download list (CC0 only), Phase 16 audio

## For the owner (about 20 to 30 minutes)

1. Log in to freesound.org with a free account.
2. For each row you want, open its URL and click **Download**. Save into `ExternalAssets/Incoming/freesound/` and **keep the default file name**, which starts with the sound id (for example `124398__alienistcog__chain-rattle-medium.aif`).
3. **Skip** any row whose page no longer says "Creative Commons 0". Never download anything marked CC-BY, CC-BY-NC or Sampling+.
4. Tick the first column (✔) of the rows you downloaded, or just tell me the ids you skipped.
5. Short on time? Download only the "Minimum" line of each group. Long recordings are meant to be sliced into variants in the A3 step, so one file can give several variants.

Notes:
- Every row was opened on its own Freesound page on 2026-11-09 and the licence column is exactly what that page showed. Nothing was downloaded from Freesound by the assistant.
- Duration is as shown on the page (m:ss.mmm). Files are mixed 44.1 / 48 / 96 kHz: A3 resamples everything to 48 kHz, mono for 3D sounds and stereo for 2D.
- Vocal rows (breath, Warden voice, whispers) are raw human or animal vocals meant to be processed into creature voices (plan section 5.4).
- Kenney CC0 covers stone, wood, grass and earth footsteps, the generic use sound, and helpers for latch, click, creak, door and cloth (see `kenney_contents.md`); these are not repeated here.

## Totals and weak spots

- Rows listed: 127 across 30 cue variant groups; every group has at least 3 candidates.
- **Step_Earth:** use the Kenney RPG footsteps first; the two Freesound rows are only a fallback.
- **Step_Metal:** Kenney has no metal footsteps; its `impactPlate_*` / `impactMetal_*` hits are a stopgap (see `kenney_contents.md`).
- **Key pickup, lever notch, door, creak, cloth, paper:** Kenney RPG Audio also has CC0 `metalLatch`, `metalClick`, `doorOpen_*`, `creak1..3`, `cloth1..4`, `bookFlip*` that can stand in if a Freesound row is skipped.
- **Warden vocals:** there are no recordings of a large creature in a stone hall; every row is a voice or animal source to be pitched down and layered (plan 5.4).
