## Major

### M1. Choosing a quality preset silently changes the resolution scale, and the slider is not refreshed
- **Evidence:**
  - `LBSettingsWidget.cpp:312-324`: the preset callback calls `SetPreset` and then `RefreshGroupRows()`.
  - `LBSettingsWidget.cpp:296-303`: `RefreshGroupRows()` refreshes the ten groups and the preset row only.
  - `LBGraphicsSettings.cpp:91` calls `SetOverallScalabilityLevel`, which runs `FQualityLevels::SetFromSingleQualityLevel` (engine `Scalability.cpp:1047`: `ResolutionQuality = GetRenderScaleLevelFromQualityLevel(...)`). With `BaseScalability.ini:37` `PerfIndexValues_ResolutionQuality="50 71 87 100 100"`, the presets give Low 50 %, Medium 71 %, High 87 % and Epic 100 %.
  - The project has no override in `Config/DefaultScalability.ini`.
- **Repro:**
  1. Start from Epic with the resolution scale at 100 %.
  2. Settings > Graphics > Quality preset, press Left. The preset becomes High; the slider still reads 100 %.
  3. Apply.
  4. Back, then reopen Settings: the slider now reads 87 %.
  5. Variant: after the preset change, press Right on the slider. It steps from the stale 100 % and overwrites the preset's value.
- **Impact:** what the screen shows differs from what is applied, and a preset change quietly lowers the render resolution.
- **Test gap:** `Functional.UI.Settings.PresetCyclesFromEpicToLow` does not check the slider.
- **Fix:** keep the slider's item index and call `SetSliderValue(Index, GetResolutionScalePercent(), false)` in the preset callback. Or, if the owner prefers presets not to touch resolution scale, read the scale before `SetPreset` and write it back after.

### M2. Rebinding conflicts ignore the fixed keys of `IMC_LB_Gameplay` (the arrows for Move, LeftControl for Crouch)
- **Evidence:**
  - `LBKeyBindings.cpp:114`: `FindKeyConflict(GetAll(LocalPlayer), ...)`. `GetAll` holds only the nine rebindable actions (`LBUILogic.cpp:113-119`), and `LBUILogic.cpp:146-160` compares against those only.
  - `Tools/ui/p18_2_assets.py:719` says the arrows, gamepad keys and Ctrl are "unnamed, fixed".
  - The IMC name table contains `Up`, `Down`, `Left`, `Right` and `LeftControl` (string scan of `Content/LastBell/Input/IMC_LB_Gameplay.uasset`).
- **Repro:**
  1. Settings > Controls > Interact, press Up. It is accepted.
  2. In play, Up both walks forward and interacts.
  3. Likewise Sprint -> LeftControl: Ctrl crouches and sprints. Candle -> Left: Left strafes and toggles the candle.
- **Impact:** R-92 says "conflicts are rejected", and GAME_DESIGN says "A key already used is refused". Neither is true for the fixed keys.
- **Fix:** add the fixed gameplay keys (Up, Down, Left, Right, LeftControl; optionally RightControl) to `KeyRefusalReason` with a message such as "That key is fixed for Move / Crouch". Move Forward may keep Up, and Crouch may keep Ctrl (same action). Add a case to `UI.Keys.RebindConflicts`.

### M3. `+DirectoriesToAlwaysStageAsNonUFS=(Path="LastBell/UI/Fonts")` will stage the 8 uncooked font `.uasset` files as loose files
- **Evidence:**
  - `Config/DefaultGame.ini` stages `LastBell/UI/Fonts` as non-UFS.
  - In UAT, `CopyBuildToStagingDirectory.Automation.cs:844-853`, the UFS branch removes `.uasset`, `.umap`, `.po` and `.archive`, but the **NonUFS branch stages every file** (`SC.StageFiles(StagedFileType.NonUFS, InputDir, StageFilesSearch.AllDirectories, OutputDir)`).
  - `Content/LastBell/UI/Fonts/` holds `FF_LB_EBGaramond_*` and `F_LB_EBGaramond_*` (8 `.uasset` files) next to `OFL.txt`.
- **Impact:** editor-format packages ship loose beside their cooked copies in the pak, at the same staged path. At best this wastes space; at worst UAT warns or refuses on a duplicate staged path. Not verified (no packaged build; R-96).
- **Fix:** move `OFL.txt` to a folder without assets (for example `Content/LastBell/Licenses/OFL_EBGaramond.txt`), stage only that path, and update R-95, TECHNICAL_DESIGN section 18 and the registry row. The `Movies` staging is correct as it is.

## Minor

### m1. The keep dialog cannot protect against a quit or crash inside its 15 s
- **Evidence:**
  - `LBGraphicsSettings.cpp:246`: `Apply` calls `UGameUserSettings::ApplySettings`, which ends in `SaveSettings()` (engine `GameUserSettings.cpp:600`). The unconfirmed mode and resolution are therefore written to `GameUserSettings.ini` before Keep.
  - Nothing at startup compares `FullscreenMode` or `ResolutionSize*` with `LastConfirmed*`.
- **Repro:**
  1. Display > a resolution the monitor shows badly > Apply.
  2. Kill the process (Alt+F4 or Task Manager) before the countdown ends.
  3. Relaunch: the game starts in the unconfirmed mode.
- **Fix:** in `ULBGameInstance::Init`, if the mode or resolution differs from the `LastConfirmed*` values, call `RevertVideoMode()` and then `ApplyResolutionSettings(false)`.
- **Docs:** TECHNICAL_DESIGN says "Keep calls Confirm() (confirms and saves)". It should say the values are already saved by Apply.

### m2. Continue or New Game can leave the player with no menu if the save subsystem refuses
- **Evidence:**
  - `LBUISubsystem.cpp:519-523`: `ClearScreens()` runs, then `ContinueGame()`. On failure there is only a log line.
  - `LBUISubsystem.cpp:495-496`: `ClearScreens()` runs, then `StartNewGame()`, which can return early (`LBSaveSubsystem.cpp:332-342`; `NewGame`'s `IsTravelTargetValid`).
- **Repro:** with the menu showing Continue, delete or corrupt `Saved/SaveGames/LB_Slot0.sav`, then choose Continue. The menu is cleared and nothing travels: an empty screen with a cursor and no way back except Alt+F4.
- **Likelihood:** low.
- **Fix:** clear the screens only after the call succeeds, or push `ELBScreen::MainMenu` again on failure.

### m3. A rebound Pause key opens the pause menu but does not close it
- **Evidence:** `LBMenuWidgetBase.cpp:818` treats only `EKeys::Escape` as Back. While a screen is open the input mode is UI-only, so `IA_Pause` (with `trigger_when_paused`) never reaches the game.
- **Repro:**
  1. Controls > Pause > P.
  2. In play, P opens the pause menu; P again does nothing. Escape closes it.
- **Fix:** in `NativeOnKeyDown`, also treat `LBKeyBindings::GetKey(owner, Pause)` as Back. Escape stays the universal Back key.

### m4. Field of view, motion blur and film grain do not preview from the pause menu
- **Evidence:** `ULBCameraFeelComponent::TickComponent` applies them (`LBCameraFeelComponent.cpp` diff, around lines 160-185), and the component does not tick while paused (no `bTickEvenWhenPaused` in `Source/`). The values change on Resume.
- **Spec:** "applies live where the engine allows". Gamma and volume do preview while paused.
- **Fix:** also apply them from an `OnUserSettingsChanged` handler, or let the component tick while paused for the post-process and FOV part only.

### m5. "Show brightness screen at next launch" acts at the next main menu of the same session
- **Evidence:** `LBUISubsystem.cpp:193-196` and `:469-478`: every `PushScreen(MainMenu)` runs the gate.
- **Repro:**
  1. In play, Settings > Accessibility > Show brightness screen at next launch: On.
  2. Pause > Return to main menu: the first-launch brightness screen appears at once, and Back is ignored.
- **Fix:** latch the gate once per process (a flag in `ULBGameInstance`), or rename the option "at the next main menu".

### m6. The loading screen is also armed for a death with no checkpoint (game -> game)
- **Evidence:** `LBGameMode.cpp:54` calls `Save->NewGame(MapName)`, which arms the screen (`LBSaveSubsystem.cpp:107-108`; the comment there admits it).
- **Spec and docs:** plan section 1.7 says "menu -> game and game -> menu" only, and GAME_DESIGN line 67 says "A reload after dying shows none".
- **Fix:** arm in `StartNewGame()` (the menu path) instead of `NewGame()`, or accept it and correct the docs.

### m7. Resolution row in Windowed fullscreen
- **What happens:** in Windowed fullscreen the Resolution row stays active and a change triggers the keep dialog, but the engine draws at the desktop size anyway (`LBSettingsWidget.cpp:411-428`; `LBGraphicsSettings.cpp:172`).
- **Fix:** disable the row in that mode (`SetItemEnabled`), or leave the resolution out of `DisplayModeDiffers` there.

### m8. Unapplied Graphics and Display changes are discarded without a word
- **When:** on a tab switch (including the Q, E, Tab and PageUp/PageDown hotkeys) and on Back.
- **Evidence:** `LBSettingsWidget.cpp:166-169` and `:259`.
- **Fix:** show a short status line or a confirm ("Discard changes?"). UX only.

### m9. `MS_LB_MenuDrift.uasset` stores an absolute local URL in its asset-registry tags
- **Evidence:** the tag section holds `Url = file://<absolute project path, including the Windows user folder>/Content/Movies/LB_MenuDrift.mp4`, next to the relative `FilePath = ./Content/Movies/LB_MenuDrift.mp4`.
- **Functional impact:** none. At runtime the URL is built from `FilePath`, relative to the project directory.
- **Why it matters:** the repo is public, and the tag leaks the local path.
- **Fix:** accept it, or re-save the asset from a neutral checkout path at release.

### m10. Test gaps
- **Untested refusals and triggers:**
  - no test covers the checkpoint-restore refusal (`HasPendingRestore`, `LBUISubsystem.cpp:333`);
  - the focus-loss pause (`HandleApplicationActivation`, hook `bPauseOnFocusLossInEditor`) has no test.
- **Persistence is not tested across a restart:** `UI.Settings.ClampsAndPersistence` reloads through `LoadConfig()` from the in-process `GConfig` (`LBUITests.cpp:238`).
- **Tests overwrite the developer's own settings:**
  - `Functional.UI.Rebind.InteractChangesPromptAndSkip` calls `LBKeyBindings::ResetAll` (`LBUIFunctionalTests.cpp:917`), which saves to the developer's `Saved/SaveGames/EnhancedInputUserSettings.sav`;
  - after the run, `Saved/Config/WindowsEditor/GameUserSettings.ini` shows `bBrightnessCalibrated=False`.
- **What to do:** add the two missing cases. For the shared files, save and restore them around the tests, or note in `MCP_NOTES` that the suite resets the developer's bindings and brightness flag.

### m11. Editor only: the gamma leaks into the editor
- **Evidence:** `ULBGameInstance::Init` (`LBGameInstance.cpp:12-15`) and the brightness slider set `GEngine->DisplayGamma`, and nothing restores it when PIE ends. The editor viewport keeps the game's gamma until restart.
- **Fix:** in `ULBGameInstance::Shutdown`, when `GIsEditor`, restore the gamma saved at `Init`.
