# P7-FIX3 — Courtyard restructure, real overgrowth, darkness & tension

## Context
After the P7-FIX2 playtest the owner said: everything else is fine, but **the courtyard still looks empty, there is no tension/spookiness, and the plants and moss can't be seen.** Diagnosis (live survey + asset research, 2026-10-25):
- **Plants are invisible by size:** the Poly Haven weeds in the level are 2–9 cm tall, nettles ≤ 22 cm, moss tufts 3–11 cm (`KV_Weed_*`, `KV_Nettle_*`, `KV_Moss_*`). 70 tiny actors in a 57 × 50 m courtyard.
- **Emptiness is layout:** the courtyard interior is X 300…6000 × Y −2500…2500; all P7-FIX2 dressing hugs the edges; the middle (X 1200–5500, Y ±900) is a bare cobble plain with a greybox fountain cube (`Fountain`, `KD_FountainPost`) and 8 isolated piers.
- **No tension:** even, calm moonlight (clear blue sky, moon 2.4 lux, fill 0.5, sky light 1.2, six 700 lm cool fills, exposure EV100 2), nothing moves, nothing occludes.
Owner decisions this round: **no storm VFX and no audio now**; **restructure + fill**; **much darker, high contrast**. Branch: continue on `claude/p7-courtyard-polish` (P7-FIX2 not merged yet; one review covers both). Rules from D-048 apply: lead plans (Opus xhigh), every implementer is spawned with `model: "sonnet", effort: "high"`; the full spec is copied to `Docs/plans/P7_FIX3_COURTYARD_RESTRUCTURE.md` for agents.

## Outcome
A ruined cloister garth: a broken arcade with arches and a half-fallen timber walk roof on the north and south, an overgrown central garden (4 kerbed beds, cross paths, a ruined well-head with a lantern at the crossing, a dead tree silhouette), dense waist-to-head-high dry grass, nettles, shrubs and creeping ivy, moss growing out of the cobble joints near every wall, and a much darker night (storm-cloud sky, deep shadows, flickering warm pools of light). Routes stay walkable and tested; GPU stays ≤ 16.6 ms at High on the RTX 4050.
